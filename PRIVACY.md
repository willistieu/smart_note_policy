# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** 25/05/2026

Welcome to **Smart Note**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Smart Note mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

- **Microphone & Audio Permission (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):**
  - The Smart Note app requires microphone access to provide the **Voice Typing** feature, which converts your spoken words into text notes.
  - **Data Processing:** We **DO NOT** store your voice recordings on any of our servers. Audio data is processed ephemerally on your device using the Android OS default speech recognition service (e.g., Google Speech Recognition).
- **Generative AI (Gemini API):**
  - The app offers a feature to polish and correct grammar in your notes using Google's Gemini AI model.
  - **Data Processing:** When you request text optimization, only the specific text you select is sent to the Google Gemini API for processing. This data is not associated with any personally identifiable information. We recommend that you do not input highly sensitive personal information (such as passwords or credit card numbers) into the AI optimization feature.
- **Local Storage:**
  - All your notes and your Gemini API Key are stored securely and locally on your device's storage using encrypted local storage mechanisms. We do not have a centralized server that collects or syncs these notes.

## 2. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. The app only utilizes official third-party APIs for its core functionalities:

- **Google Speech Services (on Android):** Used exclusively for converting speech to text.
- **Google Gemini API:** Used exclusively for processing and optimizing note text at the user's direct request.

## 3. User Rights

- You have the right to deny microphone access. If you do so, you can still use all typing and note management features manually.
- You have full control to delete or modify all your notes at any time directly within the app. All data is stored locally on your device.

## 4. Data Security

We use standard, secure storage methods provided by the Android OS to ensure your local data is safe on your device. However, securing your physical device against unauthorized access remains your responsibility.
